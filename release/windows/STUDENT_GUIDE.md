# CORTEX LOCKDOWN — CANDIDATE EXAMINATION GUIDE

**Document Version:** 1.0.0-rc1.2  
**Strictly Zero Emojis Enforced:** Yes  

---

## 1. Candidate Pre-Examination Checklist

Before launching your scheduled examination, ensure:
- [ ] You are using a 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 computer pre-authorized by your campus IT department.
- [ ] Only one physical monitor is connected. Disconnect secondary screens, HDMI cables, and wireless display mirrors.
- [ ] Your webcam and microphone are connected and functional.
- [ ] All non-essential background applications (Discord, Spotify, Teams, Zoom, OBS) are closed.
- [ ] Your computer is connected to reliable power and internet.

---

## 2. Examination Workflow (Step-by-Step)

1. **Sign In:** Navigate to your institution's Cortex portal and sign in with your student credentials.
2. **Access Assessment:** Open your active classroom and select your scheduled examination.
3. **Launch Client:** Click **"Launch Assessment in Cortex Lockdown"**. The browser will prompt to open the `cortex-lockdown://` link. Click **Open**.
4. **Readiness Check:** The application verifies single display geometry, camera presence, and process confinement.
5. **Kiosk Confinement:** The screen transitions to exclusive fullscreen mode. Standard shortcuts (Alt+Tab, WinKey) are disabled until submission.
6. **Active Testing:**
   - Answer questions and review flags as needed.
   - Answers are continuously encrypted and saved locally to prevent data loss.
   - If internet connectivity drops, continue answering normally. Your answers queue locally in encrypted storage and synchronize automatically upon network recovery.
7. **Submission:** Click **"Submit Examination"**. Upon confirmation, the application returns your immutable cryptographic submission receipt (`CORTEX-SUB-...`).
8. **Exit:** Write down your submission receipt code. The application releases the fullscreen kiosk and unlocks your desktop.
