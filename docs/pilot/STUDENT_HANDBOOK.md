## 1. GETTING STARTED

The Cortex Online Examination System delivers a reliable, distraction-free assessment environment. Candidates take assessments with automated local answer preservation, ensuring your work is protected even during intermittent network dropouts.

---

## 2. ACCOUNT ACCESS & COURSE ENROLLMENT

### 2.1 Registration & Sign-In
1. Navigate to the Cortex Student Portal (`/login`).
2. Log in using your university student email and password.
3. If creating a new account, select "Register as Student" and enter your official student identification number (Cortex ID).

### 2.2 Joining a Course
1. Obtain the 6-character course Join Code from your instructor.
2. In your Student Dashboard (`/dashboard`), click "Join Classroom".
3. Enter the code (e.g., `MH0EYQ`) and confirm. The course will appear in your enrolled subjects.

---

## 3. TAKING AN EXAMINATION

### 3.1 Pre-Exam Preparation
- Use an updated version of Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a stable laptop or desktop computer.
- Close external communication applications, chat windows, and background downloads.
- Ensure your device is plugged into power.

### 3.2 Starting Your Attempt
1. Select your course and navigate to "Examinations".
2. Read the official Examination Instructions carefully.
3. Click "Start Examination". The platform enters full assessment mode, and the countdown timer begins.

### 3.3 Answer Input & Continuous Autosaving
- Single-Choice & Multi-Select Questions: Click the corresponding radio buttons or checkboxes.
- Numerical Questions: Type exact values into the numerical input box.
- Subjective Questions: Enter written explanations into the response editor.
- Green Autosave Indicator: Each answer is preserved locally in write-ahead storage and synchronized with the edge server within 300 milliseconds.

---

## 4. NETWORK RESILIENCE & DISCONNECT POLICY

### 4.1 What Happens if Your Network Drops?
1. The connection indicator will turn amber ("Offline Mode").
2. Do not panic, refresh, or close your browser tab.
3. You can continue answering questions uninterrupted. All answers are buffered safely in your browser's encrypted local database.
4. When your internet reconnects, the platform automatically synchronizes all pending answers to the university server.

### 4.2 The 30-Second Grace Period
- If your examination timer reaches 00:00 while you are momentarily offline, you receive exactly 30 seconds for your device to deliver already-saved answers.
- You cannot edit or write new answers after time expires.

---

## 5. SUBMITTING YOUR EXAMINATION

1. When you have completed all sections, click "Submit Examination".
2. Review the confirmation dialog showing answered vs. unanswered questions.
3. Click "Confirm Submission".
4. Once submitted, your session is locked and an immutable submission receipt is recorded.
5. If immediate scoring is enabled, your objective score will appear immediately. Otherwise, results will be accessible once your instructor completes manual evaluation.
